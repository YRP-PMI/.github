# Place Manufacturing Inc.

Carleton Place, Ontario — a design and precision manufacturing shop serving
high-energy particle accelerator programs, product engineering clients, and
the snowmobile industry.

## Three practice areas under one shop

- **High-energy accelerators** — UHV-compatible fabrication, tight-tolerance
  machining, and integration work for research and industrial particle
  accelerator programs
- **Product design** — concept, mechanical design, prototyping,
  design-for-manufacture, and production hand-off
- **Snowmobile components** — specialty parts, performance components, and
  small-run assemblies for the snowmobile industry
